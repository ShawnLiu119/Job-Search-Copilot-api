# Networking and outreach patterns

Use these as structures, not scripts to copy unchanged. Every draft needs one verified personalization hook.

## Connection note

Structure: shared or relevant context + concise reason for connecting + low-pressure close.

Example:

> Hi [Name] — I’m exploring [function] roles and noticed your work on [specific area] at [Company]. My background in [relevant evidence] made your path especially interesting. I’d be glad to connect.

Keep within 300 characters. Do not ask for a referral in the connection note.

## Follow-up message

Structure:

1. Thank them for connecting.
2. State one sentence of relevant background.
3. Ask one focused question about their work, team, or transition.
4. Offer a 15–20 minute conversation while making it easy to decline.

## Email

Use a specific subject such as `Learning about [Function] at [Company]` or `[Shared context] — brief career question`.

Keep the body between 90 and 160 words. Explain why this person, not merely why the company. Ask for perspective rather than a referral. Include no attachment unless the user explicitly wants one.

## Personalization quality

Strong hooks: shared school or employer, a visible career transition, closely related functional work, a specific public talk/post/project, or a location connection.

Weak hooks: generic praise, unsupported claims about their impact, or facts that could apply to anyone at the company.
