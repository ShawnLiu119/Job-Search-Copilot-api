# Matching and resume tailoring rules

## Evidence rules

Treat resume content as evidence only when it is explicit or a conservative paraphrase. Distinguish:

- `direct`: the resume names the exact title, skill, domain, or responsibility.
- `adjacent`: the resume shows a closely related capability with a clear explanation.
- `missing`: no credible supporting evidence exists.

Do not upgrade adjacent evidence to direct evidence. Show meaningful missing requirements in the gap report.

## Title handling

Use a target headline such as `Target: Senior Product Manager` or a summary phrase when helpful. Keep historical employment titles verbatim unless the source resume already supplies an official alternate title. A parenthetical functional clarification is allowed only when truthful, for example `Consultant (Product Analytics focus)`.

## Keyword handling

Prioritize repeated and required terms from the job description. Insert a term only when an existing bullet, skill, or project supports it. Prefer natural use in the summary, skills section, and strongest relevant bullets. Avoid exact repetition merely to increase frequency.

## Bullet revision

Prefer: action + scope + method + outcome. Retain original numbers and qualifiers. If the source has no metric, improve specificity without inventing one.

## Change log

For every material edit, record:

| Original | Revised | JD requirement | Resume evidence | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use risk `low` for wording/order changes, `medium` for adjacent terminology that needs review, and `high` for any proposed edit that should not be applied without clarification. Omit high-risk text from the resume itself.

## Per-job differentiation check

Before delivery, compare all tailored variants and verify that each one has job-specific changes in at least two of these areas when truthful evidence permits:

- Target headline or professional summary
- Skills ordering and terminology
- Experience bullet ordering
- Bullet wording that surfaces a distinct requirement

Document why a variant has fewer than two material differences. Never introduce unsupported content merely to make variants look different.
